Note: this script's own printed labels ("failure survived: True", "<-- the

list. It does **not** re-check `kind`, so it cannot by itself distinguish
"persisted with full data" from "persisted as a bare, undifferentiated
name" — that distinction is what `86_87_snapshot_provenance_and_events.py`
adds (`kind: "error"`/`"done"` present, and a full restore reaching the
`onError` target). Treat this old script's output as **inconclusive on its
own** for `5e07ba8`, superseded by the new script.
