## Result

The `nested_invoke` shape now re-enters the outer state, so the call count scales with the iteration limit (`mi+3`) instead of sitting at a fixed number.

We paid particular attention to the **meta-test**, because a test-quality fix is the easiest kind to land inertly. We mutation-tested it: pinning the assertion back to a limit-independent constant makes the new meta-test **fail**, which is exactly what it is for. It has teeth.

This is worth calling out as a pattern. A pin that does not move when the limit moves is indistinguishable from a passing test until the day it matters, and ordinary review cannot tell the two apart by reading. Guarding the guard - asserting that the pin is limit-*dependent* - is what makes the original fix durable, and it is the part of this issue we would most like to see repeated elsewhere in the suite.
