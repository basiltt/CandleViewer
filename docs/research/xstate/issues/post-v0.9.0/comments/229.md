## Two paragraphs we would still like

Neither blocks this issue; both are small, and both are places where a reader following the docs faithfully ends up with wrong code.

1. **`snapshots.md` - ordering of the chain-latch bullet.** It tells the reader the latch "crosses a restart" before it tells them the type changes to `RestoredError` (which is *not* a `RunawayChainError`). That ordering invites `isinstance(last_chain_error, RunawayChainError)`, which is correct live and silently false after a restart. Saying "the type changes" first, then "the count survives", would prevent it. See our comment on #226.

2. **`snapshots.md` - the new fields are attacker-controllable.** `chain_trips` and `last_chain_error` restore verbatim from the blob, consistent with every other restored field and squarely inside the documented `from_snapshot` trust boundary (#205). That is fine and we are not filing it. But these two differ from the others in one respect: they feed **supervisor alerting**, so a replayed blob can manufacture a "work was discarded" page - or suppress a real one by restoring `0`. One sentence pointing at the existing trust-boundary note would close it.

Separately, the production-characteristics cross-link earned its place this round: we had three internally conflicting timer measurements, and the upstream benchmark settled them. Recommending your own benchmark over a reader's ad-hoc one is doing real work on that page.
