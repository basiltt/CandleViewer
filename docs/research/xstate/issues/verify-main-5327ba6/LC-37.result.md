# LC-37 / GH#37 — Verification on main @ 5327ba6 (pre-0.8.1)

## Classification: **FIXED**

`WrongThreadError` now raises eagerly, at the call site, for *any* cross-thread `send()` — both the bare (0.7.x-broken) form and the previously-correct `asyncio.run_coroutine_threadsafe(interp.send(...), loop)` idiom — and its message names the idiom explicitly and points to the replacement, `send_threadsafe()`. The behavioural break is documented in both the CHANGELOG and the `interpreters.md` guide's "Sending from Another Thread" section.
