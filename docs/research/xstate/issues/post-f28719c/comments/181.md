Confirmed fixed, and fixed on the axis that mattered.

The per-child bound holds and — the part we reopened this for — **the overrun WARNING now fires on every overrun, including the non-preemptable `def` lane**. Our round-8 report said `start(children_timeout=)` was a no-op against a plain-`def` child entry action *and suppressed its own WARNING*, so the condition was invisible. Both halves are closed.

| engine | service kind | result |
|---|---|---|
| `Interpreter` | `async def` | bounded, returns in ~0.11 s, WARNING logged |
| `Interpreter` | plain `def` | **WARNING logged**; elapsed unbounded, as documented (a `def` entry action cannot be preempted) |

The `def` row is the right outcome: the library cannot cancel a non-yielding callable, but it can and now does *tell you*. That is the difference between a documented limitation and a silent one.
