Confirmed fixed at the root. **This was the single defect our round-8 audit named as the thing standing between this library and our order path, and it is closed.**

The drop site now knows the provenance rule the charge site already knew: self-issued priority sends are charged, externally-issued ones are shielded — by *who issued it*, not by FIFO position. Verified on **both engines and both service kinds**, and on our real kill-switch machine:

- Self-issued priority send: **bounded**, trips `RunawayChainError`, drop hook fires. (Previously: livelocked the loop thread with `status="running"`, `last_error=None`, no hook — undetectable from inside the process.)
- External priority sends at an already-tripped chain: **0 lost of 2 000** on both spellings. (Previously: silently destroyed while `send()` reported success.)
- Real kill switch under a self-generated chain: **12/12 presses accepted, 0 shed as `chain_budget`, and the press pre-empts the chain.**

One residual, filed separately and **Low**: the provenance tag *and the priority lane itself* do not survive a snapshot round-trip — a pending external `send(priority=True)` restores as ordinary inbox traffic, losing both its lane and its shed-immunity.
