## Result: FIXED (new API has a gap, filed separately)

Plain and demoted events raise `TypeError`, and a genuine engine event with a patched `data` keeps its provenance, as documented. We did find one gap in the new API: `re_mint` also forwards `type=` and `src=` overrides, which lets a caller re-target a genuine completion at a *different* live invoke. Details and a standalone repro are in the new issue *"re_mint() accepts type/src overrides and can forge a completion for another live invoke"*.
