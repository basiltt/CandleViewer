Verified on `main` @ `221ce7c` (unreleased 0.8.1; `__version__` still reports
`0.8.0` — key on the commit) as part of our adoption audit (#26). CPython 3.13.7,

We reopened this narrowly last round on the grounds that the original `RealClock`
real-delay-ladder repro still exits 1. Having re-read the now-explicit contract in
`tick()`'s docstring, **we withdraw that reopen. The closure is correct and our
repro encoded a false expectation.**

`tick()` drains what is due *at the current clock reading*. No synchronous call
can make wall time pass, so a `RealClock` ladder whose later deadlines are not yet
due cannot drain in one `tick()` — and should not. Our script had encoded the
async engine's wall-clock wait as a synchronous expectation. That is our defect,
not the library's, and we have retired the script rather than re-scoring it.

The zero-delay chain and `SimulatedClock` ladder paths both drain correctly, as
they did last round. Nothing further from us on this one — thank you for stating
the contract verbatim rather than just changing behaviour to match the loudest
report.
