## Fix location

`src/xstate_statemachine/base_interpreter.py` — `_SafePlugin.__getattr__`'s
`_guarded` wrapper (~line 240-282): detects `inspect.iscoroutine(result)`
from an `async def` override, closes the coroutine (avoids the GC-time
`RuntimeWarning`), and reports it as a `TypeError` through `_report()`
(~line 284-315), which sets `interp.last_plugin_error` and fires
`on_plugin_error` on every other registered plugin. Applies uniformly to
both `SyncInterpreter` and `Interpreter` since both wrap plugins in
`_SafePlugin`.
