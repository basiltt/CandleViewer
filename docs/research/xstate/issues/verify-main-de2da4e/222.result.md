# #222 verify — de2da4e

**Classification: FIXED**

`BaseInterpreter` gained a sticky `chain_trips` counter, a
`_last_chain_error` latch exposed as `last_chain_error`, and
`clear_chain_error()`. `_note_chain_trip` is called from every
chain-budget / settle-budget trip site in both engines (stranded-actions
path, macrostep settle-storm path, sync engine's macrostep loop), and it
increments the count / sets the latch only on the FIRST cut of a trip
(`_chain_trip_open` guards repeats), notifying
`on_chain_budget_exceeded` plugins once per trip. The pre-existing
`last_error` (a per-step, `None`-on-next-clean-event view) is documented
as explicitly NOT the sticky signal, pointing callers at the new API.

All 3 cells pass; exit 0. `chain_trips` stayed at exactly 1 through all
10 benign events (no double-count for the single storm), confirming the
"once per trip" sticky semantics, and cleared to `None` without resetting
the counter.
