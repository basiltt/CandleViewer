Fixed exactly as specified. A `raise(delay=)` to the machine itself is now a debt
of the arming step (`_chain_owed_sends`, `_armed_this_step`) and its firing is
charged as engine work, so the 1 ms ping-pong cycle trips within ±1 lap of the
zero-delay `raise` cycle. Cancellation settles the debt. A delayed send issued
from *outside* an action correctly stays external and unshielded, per #192. On
`SyncInterpreter` a timer-paced self-send is caller-`tick()`-driven and has user
standing by construction, as the pin states.

## The part worth a changelog sentence

Nothing in the new charge path is **time-aware**. The chain clears only on
"raised nothing, armed nothing, owes nothing" — and a self-paced periodic
process re-arms in its own entry action, so that test is false on every lap
regardless of how much wall-clock time passed between beats.

The consequence is wider than the 1 ms ping-pong the changelog describes. A
`raise(delay=)` self-paced heartbeat or poller now **stops after `maxIterations`
beats** on the async engine:

| beat period | `f28719c` | `19cb1f1` |
|---|---|---|
| 30 ms | 114 beats, no cut | **5 beats, cut** |
| 100 ms | 34 beats, no cut | **5 beats, cut** |
| 250 ms | 15 beats, no cut | **5 beats, cut** |

(`maxIterations: 4`, 3 s budget.) **The lap count is identical at every period** —
confirming the charge is purely per-lap and independent of elapsed time. At
`maxIterations: 8` and a 30 ms beat: 47/48 beats before, **9 beats and
`RunawayChainError`** after; the sync `tick()` lane is unchanged at 30; and
external traffic every 4th sample rescues it (45 beats, clean).

We polled this to convergence over 15 s and 2 s plateaus rather than sampling —
still 9 beats — and it reproduces identically on `def` and `async def`.

**We are not filing this as a High, and here is why.** The behaviour is
*implied* by the changelog ("trips at the same lap as a zero-delay raise cycle")
and `json-config.md:110` already scopes `maxIterations` to unbroken
self-raise/self-send chains. The **documented** heartbeat idiom is unaffected —
we measured 185 and 183 beats in 6 s with **zero drops** using `after` at the
same 30 ms and `maxIterations: 8`, against 9 for the `raise(delay=)` spelling —
and no document presents self-`raise(delay=)` as a heartbeat. The failure is
loud (`RunawayChainError` on `last_error`, `on_event_dropped("chain_budget")`,
a WARNING; the machine stays `running`).

What we would ask for:

1. **Name it in the changelog as a behaviour break** for `raise(delay=)` periodic
   work, with `after` as the migration. Anyone who wrote a self-paced poller
   against 0.8.0 will find it stops silently-ish at lap N.
2. **`production-characteristics.md:95` ("a budget is not a deadline") now
   misleads** in this specific case and should be amended.
3. Consider whether the chain-clear test should acquire *any* time awareness —
   a chain whose only pending work is a timer armed 250 ms out is not the
   runaway the budget was designed to catch.

Closing the issue as fixed; the above is filed separately as a doc/scope defect.
