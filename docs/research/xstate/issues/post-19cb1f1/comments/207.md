**Two residuals, filed separately, neither blocking this close:**

1. The trip still never reaches the **error channel**: `interpreter.error` stays
   `None` and `status` stays `running`; `RunawayChainError` appears only on
   `last_error` and via `on_event_dropped(..., 'chain_budget')`. Given #207 was
   specifically about making this condition observable, the error channel seems
   like the natural home for it.
2. `last_error` is **cleared once settled on the `async def` lane and retained on
   the `def` lane**. A supervisor that samples `last_error` asynchronously rather
   than per-step therefore sees the runaway on `def` services and **misses it on
   the recommended `async def` ones**. This one bit us, and it is why our own
   error hooks now sample `on_event_dropped(..., 'chain_budget')` instead.

Closing.
