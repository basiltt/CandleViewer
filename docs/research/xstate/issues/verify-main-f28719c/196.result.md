## Notes on the reporter's own withdrawal

The original report explicitly withdrew its "permanent starvation" claim
and the strongest form of its parity claim after its own refutation (a
12s settle drains 300/300; the trigger chart is one the docs name as an
invalid `RunawayChainError` cause). This verification used a generous
drain-detection loop (poll until both the inbox and priority queues are
empty, up to 15s) rather than a fixed sleep, confirming the queues do fully
drain and `EXT` is never silently lost while `last_error` stays clean — the
narrower, surviving concern from the report. Both service kinds converge
on `RunawayChainError` (parity), matching the CHANGELOG's stated fix scope
(the eventless-transition selection fix), not a claim that this shape stops
being a runaway (it remains one, correctly, per the docs).
