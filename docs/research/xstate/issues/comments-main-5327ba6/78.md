## Ride-along finding — M-6 (Low): error precedence before `start()`

One small edge in the new guardrail. Called on an interpreter that has not been
started, `send_threadsafe()` has two things to complain about — the unknown
event / rejected payload, and the fact that the interpreter is not running — and
which one surfaces depends on lifecycle phase rather than on a stated rule.

Not a correctness problem (both are errors and both are loud), but a caller
writing `except UnknownEventError` around a startup race will see the other one.
Worth either fixing the precedence deliberately (validate first, then
lifecycle — the validation is pure and cheap) or documenting which wins.
