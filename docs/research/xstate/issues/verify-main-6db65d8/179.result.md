## Root cause of fix (file:line)

`src/xstate_statemachine/interpreter.py`:
- `_publish_completion()` (~L2683) — the single charged lane every completion
  (plain-`def`, `async def`, child-actor terminal, `error.platform`) now goes
  through, calling `_deliver_priority(event, engine_completion=True)`.
- `_deliver_priority()` (~L2370-2386) — `engine_completion=True` charges
  `_raise_depth` and settles `_chain_owed` unconditionally, regardless of
  `_processing`, closing the WHEN→WHO gap that let the coroutine lane escape
  charging.
- `_owe_completion()` (~L440) / `_chain_owed` (declared in `__slots__` at
  L275, init at L422) — keeps a step's chain open until its armed coroutine
  service's completion lands, so "raised nothing, armed nothing" correctly
  decides chain-end without letting independent one-deep completions
  accumulate.
- Call sites routed through `_publish_completion`: L2519, L2555, L2654,
  L2854, L2871, L2995, L3040, L3063 — covers plain services, coroutine
  services, and child-actor `onDone`/`onError`.
