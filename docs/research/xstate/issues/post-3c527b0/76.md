**Disposition: confirm closed.**

On criterion 4, one hole we have filed separately rather than reopening here: the compatibility shim treats a `**kwargs` catch-all in `set_timeout`'s signature as *consent* to receive `sync=`. A clock written against the 0.8.0 `Clock` protocol that declares `**kwargs` for its own unrelated reasons is handed an argument it has never heard of, and fails with `AssertionError: unexpected kwargs: ['owner', 'sync']` from `base_interpreter.py:3451`. The recommendation is that the inspection require the `sync` parameter **by name** rather than accepting a catch-all as agreement.
