Re-confirmed on this commit at **500-way concurrency** with `actionErrorPolicy: "rollback"` + `onUnhandled: "defer"` + a bounded `RAISE` inbox armed simultaneously — all 500 resolve, none hang. #43's one-task-per-child redesign in this PR did not disturb receipt resolution at all.

The root cause was fixed structurally rather than patched: `Interpreter._detach()` gives the queued envelope its own identity at the send boundary, so caller-side object reuse cannot collide.

**Disposition: confirm closed.** This retired a constraint on our side — we no longer require fresh-`Event` discipline in our own code, because the library does the work.
