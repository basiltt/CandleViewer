Both engines now cut at the same link (1001). Implementation: action-side `send()` appends to `Interpreter._internal_queue` and increments `_raise_depth` (`interpreter.py`, ~1452-1453), checked at `:1173` and reported at `:1185`, mirroring the `raise` built-in.

**One consequence of this reroute is a separate defect, filed on its own with a repro**, and we want to flag it here because it lives in this fix's own code path: the reroute gates on the interpreter-wide `_processing` flag, so an *external* `send()` arriving while a macrostep is in flight is also routed into the internal queue and charged to the self-raised chain budget. At `burst=1500` we measured **1 accepted external event lost**, with `on_event_dropped(reason='chain_budget')` and `last_transition_ok=True` (control burst of 999: 0 lost). The distinction needed is provenance/origin, not `_processing`. That does not reopen #90 - it is a new root cause introduced by the reroute.

No regressions observed against the earlier verification sets, the full suite (3 276 passed / 0 failed, coverage 90%) or our probe baseline.
