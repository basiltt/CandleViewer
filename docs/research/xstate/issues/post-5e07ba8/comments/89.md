One behaviour change worth a release-note line rather than a fix: un-introspectable callables previously defaulted to *accepting*, and now default to *not accepting*. That is exactly what the issue asked for, and it is the safe direction, but it is a silent behaviour flip for anyone passing a C-implemented or heavily-wrapped clock.
