## Residual — correction to this verify pass (independent recheck)

The `nested_invoke` shape in `209_lap_parity_sweep_1_25.py` (and identically
in `tests/test_round9_findings.py::TestLapParityAtEveryLimit._nested`) is
**not actually a runaway/cycling shape** and contributes no real coverage of
the #209 parity fix. Its `onDone` targets (`#m0.a.b` from `a.a`'s invoke,
`#m0.a.a` from `b`'s invoke) are both internal to the compound state `a`
that owns invoke `i1` — the transitions never exit/re-enter `a` itself, so
`i1` fires exactly once, `i2` (owned by leaf `a.b`) fires exactly once, and
the machine parks permanently in `m0.a.a` with 2 total calls. Confirmed by
direct probe: calls are pinned at exactly 2 for every `mi` in {1, 100, 1000},
independent of `maxIterations`, and `last_error` is `None` (no
`RunawayChainError` ever trips). This means all 25 `nested_invoke` rows in
the "50 cells" sweep pass **trivially** (2==2==2 by construction, not
because the lap-counting logic agrees under load) and don't stress
anything #209 was about. Recommend fixing the shape (e.g. target a state
outside `a`, or give `a.a`/`a.b` their own invokes that force `a` to be
exited and re-entered) so it actually reaches `maxIterations`, or drop it
from the sweep as redundant with `rollback_ondone`.

This does **not** overturn the FIXED classification: the shape named in the
changelog ("rollback + onDone") is `rollback_ondone`, and that shape *does*
genuinely scale with `maxIterations` (2, 3, 4, ... 14 calls across limits
1-25) and agrees across all three lanes at every limit — the acceptance
criterion is met on the shape that actually exercises the fix. The
`nested_invoke` half of the sweep is inert padding, not a false-positive on
the criterion itself, but the verify script's "50 cells" framing overstates
coverage and should be corrected/flagged.
