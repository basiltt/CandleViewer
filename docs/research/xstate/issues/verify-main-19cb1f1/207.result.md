## Original repro (post-f28719c/new/repro)

`R9-07_rollback_ondone_silent_wedge.py` still exits **1** ("SILENT WEDGE at
default maxIterations") on this build — but this is the reporter's own
repro under-waiting, not a residual defect. Its own driver waits only
`15 * 0.03s = 0.45s` after `send("GO", wait=True)` returns (which itself
returns near-instantly — under 10ms — because the self-feeding chain runs
in the background after the initial `send` call, not synchronously inside
it). The chain takes **~0.48–0.52s of real wall time** to reach the
default 1000-lap limit and trip `RunawayChainError`. Reproduced directly:
with the identical chart and a 0.45s wait, `last_error` is still the
entry action's bare `RuntimeError` (boom count ~460-520, i.e. mid-chain);
extending the wait to converge (`for _ in range(60): sleep(0.03)` =
1.8s, or explicit convergence-polling) always yields boom_count=1001 and
`last_error=RunawayChainError`. Verified deterministic across 6+ repeated
runs at the longer wait — never once wedges silently once the chain has
had time to finish.
