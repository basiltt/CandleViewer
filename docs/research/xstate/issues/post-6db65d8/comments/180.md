# Comment for issue #180 — external priority sends are charged to the chain budget

**Action:** comment — **the filed half is fixed; the opposite half is open and is a Blocker**
**Disposition:** **FIXED on the charge side; REGRESSION on the shed side (new issue filed)**

---

Verified on `main` @ `6db65d8` (merge of PR #191, `fix/0.8.1-round7`; unreleased
0.8.1) as part of our adoption audit (#26). Build identified **by commit** —
`__version__` still reports `0.8.0` on this tree. CPython 3.13.7, Windows 11,
fresh venv. Every claim below was reproduced in a clean process before it was
written down, and **every service-bearing check was run with both `def` and
`async def` services**.

**The half this issue was filed for is fixed and verified.** External
`send(..., priority=True)` is no longer charged: 0 drops for both `priority=True` and
`priority=False` in the filed repro, and 50 concurrent external priority sends into an
open macrostep on a real control machine give 0 dropped / 0 `chain_budget` refusals on
both service spellings. The provenance-by-WHO rule is correct where it was applied.

**Fixed as filed; narrower than claimed — it was applied on one side of the ledger
only, and that is our round-8 Blocker, filed separately as R8-01.** `_deliver_priority` consults provenance when deciding whether to
**charge** (`interpreter.py:2342-2389`, keyed on `engine_completion`) and nothing
consults it when deciding whether to **shed** (`:1598`, `over = self._raise_depth >
limit`), over a single FIFO `_priority_queue` that carries external sends and engine
completions together. Consequences, both reproduced fresh at this commit on both
spellings:

* a `send(priority=True)` **issued from an action** is never charged — `engine_completion`
  is `False` for every public priority send whatever the issuer — and livelocks
  unbounded with `status="running"`, `last_error is None`, no drop hook, and `start()`
  never returning. The identical machine on the plain lane is bounded at 27 laps with
  `RunawayChainError` + a `chain_budget` drop.
* a genuinely **external** priority event sitting at the head of the FIFO when an
  unrelated self-generated chain trips is **destroyed** with `reason="chain_budget"` —
  8–9 of 2 000, with `send()` accepted and `last_error is None`. The `priority=False`
  control loses 0.

The non-priority `send()` path already does this correctly at `:898-908` via
`_issued_from_own_action()`. Suggested direction: tag provenance on the queued item at
enqueue time (engine-completion / self-raised / external), charge the first two, and
make the shed test refuse to drop anything tagged external — or give external priority
sends a FIFO the chain-budget axe cannot reach.

Please do not cut 0.8.1 until this lands; it is the regression surface of this very
fix, and on our side it is the kill-switch and cancel lane.

Thank you — round-8 scorecard and the full write-up are in #26.
