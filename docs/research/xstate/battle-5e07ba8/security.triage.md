## Summary

All five filed defects (D-security-1 through D-security-5) reproduce exactly
as described, with source citations confirmed to match the current `5e07ba8`
checkout and, for the two GitHub-hosted claims (D-security-2, D-security-3),
independently re-queried live via `gh api` / `grep` rather than trusted from
the original report. No severity changes: nothing here sits on the order-entry
or state-corruption path, so none qualify as Blocker or High under the OMS
scale. D-security-1 is the one item worth carrying forward as an explicit,
written rule (not just an observation) in any plugin-usage guidance for this
library, since it is the only defect with a direct secrets-in-production
failure mode if copy-pasted as shown in the library's own docs.
