# E24-K01 — Liquidation-zone estimation methodology and its honesty limits

Branch `spike/e24-liq-zone-methodology`. Artefacts: `E24-K01-harness/` (stdlib Python, deterministic),
`E24-K01-report.json`. Architect review: owner approval pending.

## 1. Recommendation (decision)

1. **Ship only method (b), the realised-liquidation histogram**, as the primary and only default surface.
   It is exact (recorded forced flow), not an estimate.
2. **Method (a), the OI-weighted leverage-bucket projection, is descoped** from US-DERIV-007's "estimated
   zones" overlay. Disposition: *descoped pending evidence* — it rests on assumed inputs
   (leverage mix is not published; section 4). This rests on input honesty only, not on the synthetic
   hit rates: in the matched-mix sensitivity run (section 3) (a) does beat chance, which shows the
   synthetic numbers cannot settle the question either way. **E24-S07 must be re-scoped** to the histogram; it
   must not build (a). Re-open only if a re-run on real recorded symbol-days (E24-X01/E07 recorder output)
   shows (a) beats chance with a stated margin, and then only as an opt-in overlay.
3. Feature is **off by default**. Defaults for (b): bucket = 20 ticks, top-5 buckets, lookback = session.
4. Method (c) hybrid collapses to (b) given 2.

## 2. Honest limits of this spike (read first)

No recorded `liquidations` / `open_interest` symbol-days exist in the repo (`packages/fixtures` has only a
synthetic sample), and tests/spikes may not call the exchange (C-13.5). The backtest therefore runs on
**three seeded synthetic symbol-days** (two calm, one cascade) whose hidden leverage mix (15x/30x/75x)
deliberately differs from the assumed tiers (10/25/50/100x). Consequences:
- Results validate the harness and decision rule; they are **not evidence about Bybit**. The generator
  biases outcomes: with a mismatched mix (a) is handicapped, with a matched mix it is flattered, and (b)
  is flattered either way because the synthetic liquidations cluster by construction. The descoping of (a)
  rests on the *input honesty* argument (section 4) only.
- **AC1 deviation (not self-waived):** Scenario "at least three recorded symbol-days" is NOT met. An
  owner-approved exception is requested on issue #664; until granted, AC1 stays open and the numbers
  here are provisional. Closing it needs E07 recorder output re-run through this harness.
- Required follow-up (owner/E24-S07 gate): re-run `liq_zone_eval.py` against >=3 real recorded symbol-days
  incl. a cascade before any estimated overlay is reconsidered.

## 3. Results (synthetic; `E24-K01-report.json`)

Hit = a zone has a recorded liquidation within N=40 ticks in the next M=60 min; false-positive = 1 - hit.
Hourly evaluation 02:00-23:00 on three days.

| Method | Params | Hit rate | False-positive rate | Zones scored |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) OI leverage buckets | tiers 10/25/50/100x, MMR 0.5% (assumed) | 0.042 | 0.958 | 504 |
| (b) Realised histogram | 20-tick buckets, top 5, causal (past only) | 0.137 | 0.863 | 315 |
| Chance baseline | 8 uniform zones within +/-5% of ref, seed 7 | 0.083 | 0.917 | 504 |

Sensitivity, hidden leverage mix = assumed tiers (10/25/50/100x), three other seeded days:
(a) 0.137, (b) 0.213, chance 0.087. So (a) beats chance when its assumption happens to be true, which is
exactly what cannot be known on real data. Both runs are synthetic; neither is a result about Bybit.
(b) is causal (uses only liquidations before the evaluation hour).

## 4. Input provenance

| Input | Status |

| `open_interest` (aggregate) | exchange-published |

| Reference price | exchange-published mark/last |

## 5. Failure modes

(a): wrong leverage mix shifts every zone; cross vs isolated margin, hedging and manual deleveraging are
invisible; zones look precise but are invented. (b): sparse symbols give empty/noisy buckets; past clusters
do not guarantee future ones (survivorship after a cascade clears the book); late/missing recorder data.

## 6. Cost budget (for E24-S07)

Measured in this harness (pure Python, cascade day, 1500 liquidations, 573 buckets; indicative only, the
production implementation will differ):
- Method (b) full recompute from all events: ~1.19 ms per update (worst case, upper bound).
- Method (b) incremental (O(1) bucket increment + top-5 sort of all buckets): ~0.0035 ms per liquidation.
- Method (a), descoped, for comparison: ~0.006 ms per update.
Recommendation: use the incremental path; the full recompute alone already fits within the SCR-054
2 ms/frame scripting limit but leaves little headroom, so S07 must not recompute per frame and must record
its own production benchmark. Suggested metrics: `orderflow_liq_hist_recompute_seconds`,
`orderflow_liq_hist_buckets`.

## 6a. Draft SCR-058 methodology text (for E24-D03)
